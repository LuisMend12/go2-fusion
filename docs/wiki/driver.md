# Driver

The ROS 2 driver that connects to the Go2, publishes its sensors and forwards commands to it.

Part of: [Oak](../../README.md)

## Pages

- [Go2 driver](go2-driver.md) - The `go2_robot_sdk` package: the driver node, launch files and configs that turn the Go2 into a ROS 2 robot.
- [Robot link](robot-link.md) - How the driver talks to the dog: WebRTC over Wi-Fi (the lab default, with AES-key support for firmware 1.1.15+) or CycloneDDS over Ethernet.
- [Go2 interfaces](go2-interfaces.md) - The custom ROS 2 message types used between the driver and the other packages.
